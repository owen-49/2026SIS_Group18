02 · The problemClaimTrace

The last 48 hours go into checking citations, not writing the paper.

Untrusted

Co-authors’ references are taken on faith.

A Related Work section written by three people contains references nobody has opened.

Interrupted

Every check means leaving the editor.

Overleaf → Zotero → a PDF reader → back. The cost is not the click, it is the context switch.

Fabricated

Machine-written references look real.

Wrong venue, wrong year, or a DOI that resolves to a different paper entirely.

All three personas asked for the same thing: the original evidence, not just a conclusion.

03 · What it doesThree surfaces, one evidence trail

Three checks, in the place
you already write.

01 / AUDIT

Audit

every reference, every field

Each bibliography entry is checked against OpenAlex, then Crossref. Title, authors, year and venue are compared field by field.

  • Identifier first, title only as fallback
  • Five states — never a silent “not found”
  • Works on .bib files and whole PDFs
02 / VERIFY

Verify

claim vs. what the paper says

A selected sentence is matched to the cited paper, the relevant passages are retrieved, and the judgement ships with its evidence.

  • Four verdicts, every one evidenced
  • Every verdict carries the passage
  • Bring your own model — no server key
03 / READ

Read

the paper as it was written

PDFs become structured Markdown — formulas, tables and figures preserved — and render inside the review pane.

  • Hybrid parsing, local fallback
  • Page-anchored paragraphs
  • Hover a citation in Overleaf

06 · VerifyClaim × cited source

The verdict is only the headline.
The passage is the argument.

Claim 01

“The paper reports that the method reduced carbon emissions by forty percent.”

Not found The source was read and does not contain this claim. That is different from failing to read it.
Claim 02

“Self-attention relates different positions of a single sequence.”

“Self-attention, sometimes called intra-attention, is an attention mechanism relating different positions of a single sequence in order to compute a representation of the sequence.”

Support Retrieved from the cited paper · quoted verbatim.

Every comparison ships the retrieved passages, their rank and similarity score, and the page the passage came from when the parser recorded one. A model that ran is not a model that judged — COMPARED is the only status that carries a verdict.
