01 / WHAT IT DOES
01 / AUDIT

Audit

every reference, every field

Every bibliography entry is checked against OpenAlex, then Crossref. Title, authors, year and venue are compared field by field — and a lookup that failed stays visibly failed.

02 / VERIFY

Verify

claim vs. what the paper says

A selected sentence is matched to the cited paper. The relevant passages are retrieved and every verdict ships with the passage, its rank and its similarity score.

03 / READ

Read

the paper as it was written

PDFs become structured Markdown — formulas, tables and figures preserved — and render inside the review pane, next to the manuscript.

A reference is either checked, or it is visibly not checked.
